About

Born in 2020 from the dust of an abandoned drive-in and the static of a forgotten satellite, Lost Endeavours blend surf-rock twang with horror-flick atmosphere. Three musicians, one mission — bring sounds from outer space back to earth: loud, reverb-soaked, and a little unhinged.
